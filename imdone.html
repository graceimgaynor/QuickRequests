PVI Performance Advisor: value created from a real business challenge

The project began with a clear operational problem: reviewing PVI data across portfolios required repetitive manual entry, was slow, and created opportunities for human error. I helped turn that need into a structured calculator and advisor experience designed for clearer conversations, faster analysis, and responsible scenario planning.

What the tool improves

  • Brings partner, portfolio, fiscal-month, category, and metric context into one view.
  • Separates official loaded PVI values from unofficial projections and what-if scenarios.
  • Explains score drivers, gaps to the next threshold, and potential areas for discussion.
  • Reduces dependence on repeatedly re-keying values across portfolio views.
  • Makes a complex methodology easier to explain to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Why it matters to TD SYNNEX

  • Partner experience: supports clearer, more consistent conversations about current position and possible next steps.
  • Operational discipline: creates a repeatable workflow with validation, documentation, and visible guardrails.
  • Decision support: helps teams focus attention on PVI drivers and rebate thresholds without presenting modeled values as official results.
  • Scalability: provides a foundation that can be tested, improved, and reused rather than remaining a one-off spreadsheet exercise.

I listened to a difficult workflow, learned an unfamiliar Cisco program, and built toward a usable solution. My strength is not simply producing analysis. It is connecting data, user needs, business rules, communication, and governance so the result can become a practical product.
