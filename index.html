I am requesting a consolidated enterprise development environment to support my current and planned analytics projects, building on the econometric foundation from my completed graduate thesis. These include demand-elasticity modeling for geographic antitrust market definition, early-career attrition analysis, LinkedIn Learning engagement analytics, an attrition and mobility Power BI dashboard, a Partner Value Index calculator, pricing and diversion modeling, and an analytics developer portfolio. The requested environment includes a developer-ready Windows workstation (32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD, dual monitors), Visual Studio Code, Python, Node.js, React development tools, Git, approved GitHub access, Power BI Desktop, API development and testing tools, and approved Python/JavaScript package access. Where cloud, AI, container, database, or third-party tools require additional governance, I am requesting formal review and role-based access rather than unrestricted permissions. This consolidated setup would allow me to turn research and one-time analyses into reproducible analytics workflows, governed dashboards, documented calculation tools, internal applications, and responsible AI-enabled prototypes while reducing repeated individual software requests. Alongside the environment request, I am pursuing a structured six-month learning plan (Python fundamentals, database design, Power BI, and AI agent/Copilot development) leading toward AI-900, DP-900, PL-300, and AI-102 certifications, so that the tooling and skill-building progress together.
